Smoking :

NON Smoking:
Non smoking = CALCULATE(COUNT('health_dataset 23'[Patient_ID]),'health_dataset 23'[Smoking_Status] = "Never") 
Male Count:
Male Patients = CALCULATE(COUNT('health_dataset 23'[Patient_ID]),'health_dataset 23'[Gender] = "Male")
Female Count :
  Female Patients = CALCULATE(COUNT('health_dataset 23'[Patient_ID]),'health_dataset 23'[Gender] = "Female")

AVERAGE YEARS of  SMOKING:
